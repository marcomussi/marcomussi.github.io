Marco Mussi

Assistant Professor, Politecnico di Milano

I am Assistant Professor with the Dipartimento di Elettronica, Informazione e Bioingegneria, in the Artificial Intelligence and Robotic Laboratory of Politecnico di Milano. My research focuses on artificial intelligence and machine learning, particularly on foundational aspects of online learning and reinforcement learning.
